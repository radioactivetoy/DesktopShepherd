# DesktopShepherd 1.0 — Release Candidate 3

New languages, new widget features, a memory fix for "Now playing", and DesktopShepherd now
steps aside while you play full-screen games.

## Download

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| **DesktopShepherd-Setup-1.0.0-rc.3.exe** | Installer. Installs for your user only, no administrator rights. Running it over an older version updates it and keeps your boxes. New: `--silent` installs without any window. |
| **DesktopShepherd-Portable-1.0.0-rc.3.zip** | Portable copy. Unzip anywhere and run; settings stay in a `data` folder next to the program. |
| **SHA256SUMS.txt** | Checksums of the files above (the built-in updater checks them before installing). |

Windows may warn about an *unknown publisher* (the program isn't code-signed yet): choose
**More info → Run anyway**.

## New

- **Four more languages:** French, German, Italian and Portuguese (with dates in each). The
  program follows your Windows language, or pick one from the new drop-down list in Settings.
  Native speakers: suggestions for better wording are welcome as a GitHub issue.
- **World clock:** add other cities to the clock (right-click the box → *Widget settings…*), from
  Windows' own list of time zones, daylight saving included. Each shows its time and the
  difference to yours, with a moon when it's night there.
- **Network speed** in the system monitor: download and upload speed with a small graph.
- **Now playing:**
  - **Volume and mute** next to the play buttons (shown while the mouse is over the box).
  - **Seek:** click or drag the progress bar to jump within the song.
  - **Sound output:** right-click the box to see the current speakers or headphones and open
    Windows' picker to switch.
- **Bluetooth batteries** in Laptop status: headphones, mice and keyboards that report their
  battery to Windows.
- **Pause while gaming:** while a full-screen app or game runs (borderless included), the boxes on
  that monitor stop updating and DesktopShepherd's keyboard shortcuts are released for the game;
  boxes on your other monitors keep going. Also pauses while the screen is off or the PC is
  locked. (Setting: *Pause while a full-screen app or game is running*, on by default.)
- The settings window shows the version next to the name.

## Fixed

- **Memory growth with "Now playing":** about 5 MB an hour while music was playing. It now uses
  one long-lived connection to Windows' media service. Laptop status and the drive-space reading
  got the same treatment. Measured over hours: memory now stays flat.
- **Box text colour after wallpaper changes:** boxes that were hidden when the wallpaper changed
  (screen off, locked, covered, another page) now pick the right text colour as soon as they show.
- **Widgets stayed in front of windows** after a click — they now stay behind your apps.
- **Play button** invisible on bright wallpapers (dark symbol on the dark circle).
- **Duplicate icon** after copying a file, deleting the original and renaming the copy to the
  original's name.
- The drop shadow and *Raised look* options (per box, in *Personalize*) are tuned: the raised look
  is a soft bevel all round, with smooth corners.

## Known limitations

- Not code-signed yet (the *unknown publisher* warning).
- Bluetooth devices that don't report their battery to Windows don't show one (same as in
  Windows' own settings).
- Switching the sound output uses Windows' own picker (Windows has no official way for other
  programs to switch it).
